# Technology logo assets

These logos identify tools used in Mohamad Moallem's work. They are hosted locally;
the portfolio does not request images from third-party servers at runtime.

`sources.json` lists the exact upstream URL, revision, collection, license, and any
modification for every asset.

- **Devicon:** original SVG variants from the official
  [devicons/devicon repository](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), pinned to
  revision `32fe1d3b3f36ad11f90ab94f7db59738139c2bf6`. The MIT license is preserved in
  `DEVICON-LICENSE.txt`. The original artwork is unchanged.
- **Simple Icons:** SVG marks from the official
  [simple-icons/simple-icons repository](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons).
  Its CC0-1.0 notice is preserved in `SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`. Marks are filled
  with a solid color; the geometry is unchanged. Archived releases are used for
  Tableau, Microsoft Excel, and Power BI, which are absent from the current set.
- **Unsloth:** the unmodified 128px application icon from the official
  [unslothai/unsloth repository](https://github.com/unslothai/unsloth), pinned in
  `sources.json`. Its upstream repository notices are preserved in
  `UNSLOTH-LICENSE.txt` and `UNSLOTH-COPYING.txt`.

Names and logos remain the property of their respective owners. Their presence
identifies technologies and does not imply endorsement or affiliation.

The Firebase suite mark also identifies Firestore, the Hugging Face mark also
identifies Transformers, and the Ultralytics mark identifies YOLO. Semantic skills
such as statistical modeling and natural language processing use Lucide symbols
instead of brand marks; model names without a distinct logo also use symbols.
